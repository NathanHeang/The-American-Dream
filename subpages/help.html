Controls
Keybinds Function
W or ↑ Move up
A or ← Move left
S or ↓ Move down
D or → Move right
Space Jump
Shift Sprint
Esc Pause
Left or right click Be able to interact with specific objects or events depending on whether it's left or right click.


Goal

The basic goal of the game is to save enough money to get through the next day in a week by going to work, completing tasks on time, and overcome the random effects that happen during the game. Good luck!
