Goal

The basic goal of the game is to save enough money to get through the next day in a week by going to work, completing tasks on time, and overcome the random effects that happen during the game while also providing for your family and getting through your bucket list. Good luck!

When you get to the workplace, you will come across an room of computers. Go to your assigned computer where your boss will e-mail you tasks you must do in time to meet expectations for the day. Be prepared for any random events that may occur on your computer. Otherwise, it's gonna be your responsibility for the damages.
