America is a Land Where Its People Dream to Succeed Through Determination.

Make money working your day job in the city and provide for your family!


But Dreams Are Just Dreams.

You follow the perspective of a struggling family man juggling through the bucket list of bills and groceries, but as the days go by, more and more unlucky events arrive throughout the city to ruin your day. Can you survive one week living the lifestyle people thought was "The American 'Dream'"?


Age Rating

This game is intended for ages 13 and above.


No AI was used in the creation of this project.

This means all textures are hand-drawn and all programming is human!
