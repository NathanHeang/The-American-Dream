The American "Dream" is a chaotic and overexaggerated game about the financial struggles of the average American male. You follow an struggling person with an wife and kids, trying to juggle through the bucket list, such as paying the bills and buying the groceries. As the game progress, it gets more tense and random, creating an unpredictable and high-pased experience for the player.

This game is meant for ages 13+ (teenagers) but anyone underage are absolutely allowed to play too!
